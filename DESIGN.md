# Design System: Giriraaj Textiles

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "Precision Engineered"**

The design philosophy marries industrial manufacturing reliability with the sleek, high-end technical execution of modern software companies like Air.inc, Stripe, and Linear. The aesthetic is heavily choreographed, relying on precise scroll-driven sequences to tell the story of the manufacturing process, supported by a deliberate, multi-role full color palette. It explicitly rejects the overly rounded, floating-blob "AI slop" style, as well as the garish neon glows of Web3 landing pages.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Structural, geometric, and clean.
- Highly choreographed scroll-driven motion.
- Full, deliberate color palette replacing generic monochrome.
- Authoritative, established tone.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use precise, 1px structural borders to define spatial relationships, akin to technical schematics.
- **Do** implement highly choreographed, scroll-driven reveals to tell the process story.
- **Do** treat product specification data with the typographic care of a data-table or spec-sheet.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use generic "AI slop" (floating blobs, neon glows, rounded pills).
- **Don't** design consumer-style e-commerce layouts.
- **Don't** use generic neon Web3 styling.
- **Don't** build traditional, boring corporate layouts that feel outdated.
