Giriraj Textiles — IA Concepts

10 structural wireframes exploring different ways to organize and present an industrial synthetic yarn manufacturer.

01. The Industrial Catalog

E-commerce style grid focusing on rapid product discovery and batch sampling.

02. Technical Datasheet First

Engineering credibility. Immediate drop into a massive, highly structured spec table.

03. Supply Reliability First

Combating supply chain fear with live-looking dispatch schedules and QA steps.

04. Application-Driven

Focuses entirely on the end-use (Cement, Fertilizer) to route buyers to the right yarn.

05. The Material Flow

Linear, timeline-based process visualization from raw material to dispatch.

06. Industrial Editorial

Asymmetric grid, huge typography, and massive negative space. Leadership positioning.

07. The Quick-Sample Flow

Split-screen layout prioritizing lead generation above all else. The homepage IS the form.

08. The Dispatch Board

Brutalist layout modeled after an airport departure board showing ready stock.

09. The Specification Index

No hero, no fluff. Just an A-Z data index of all specs and immediate download links.

10. The Command Center

Dashboard-style layout styled for reading technical documents with a persistent sidebar.
