Our Origin

Born from Gujarat's industrial soil

Giriraj Textiles began in 1992 as a single-extrusion unit in Rajkot — a city known for its engineering spirit and manufacturing heritage. What started as polypropylene tape yarn for local weaving units has grown into a 12,000-ton annual manufacturing operation.

Three decades later, we remain rooted in the same soil, serving over 200 clients across 18 Indian states. Our growth has been organic — built on consistency, trust, and the quiet discipline of doing one thing well.

Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our standard MOQ is 500 kg per denomination. For custom blends and specialized deniers, the MOQ may vary depending on production setup requirements. Contact our sales team for specific requirements.

What is the typical lead time for orders?

Standard products are dispatched within 72 hours of order confirmation. Custom formulations and large bulk orders may require 5–7 working days depending on production scheduling.

Do you provide technical data sheets?

Yes. Every product line has a comprehensive TDS covering tensile strength, elongation, shrinkage, denier tolerance, and recommended end-use parameters. Request via email for the latest revisions.

Can you develop custom yarn formulations?

Absolutely. Our R&D team works closely with clients to develop bespoke yarn specifications — from denier and tenacity to color masterbatch integration. Minimum runs apply for custom development.

Which states do you supply to?

We currently supply to 18 states across India including Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Punjab, West Bengal, and Uttar Pradesh. Logistics partnerships ensure reliable pan-India delivery.
