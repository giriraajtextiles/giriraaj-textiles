Common Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Standard MOQ is 500 kg per denomination. Custom blends may require higher minimums depending on setup complexity.

Do you ship pan-India?

Yes. We supply to 18 states via partnered transport networks. Standard delivery is 3–5 working days from dispatch.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Sample cones (2–5 kg) are available for evaluation. Contact sales with your specifications to request samples.

Do you provide test certificates?

Every shipment includes a batch test certificate covering tenacity, elongation, denier, and shrinkage values.

What payment terms do you offer?

Standard terms are advance or against delivery. Credit terms available for established clients with proven track record.

Can you match competitor specifications?

Send us the spec sheet or a sample cone. Our technical team will develop an equivalent or improved formulation.
