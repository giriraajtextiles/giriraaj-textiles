Giriraj Textiles — Manufacturing Philosophy

01

Polymer Selection

Raw polymer granules sourced from certified suppliers, tested for molecular weight distribution and melt flow index.

02

Extrusion

Precision temperature-controlled extrusion through spinnerets, forming continuous filaments with consistent denier.

03

Drawing & Texturing

Controlled stretching aligns molecular chains, developing tensile strength. Texturing adds bulk and elasticity.

04

Quality & Dispatch

Every batch tested for tenacity, elongation, and shrinkage. Cones packed and dispatched within 72 hours.

Three decades of growth

1992

Foundation

Giriraj Textiles established in Rajkot with a single extrusion line producing polypropylene tape yarn.

2003

Expansion

Added polyester yarn manufacturing. Facility expanded to 40,000 sq ft with 6 extrusion lines.

2014

Diversification

Launched monofilament and fibrillated yarn lines. Reached 8,000 ton annual capacity milestone.

2024

Today

12,000 ton annual capacity, 200+ clients across 18 states. ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing.
