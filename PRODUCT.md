# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
B2B clients: businesses that consume the thread directly in their manufacturing processes, and distributors who resell it. They are looking for specifications, reliability, and clear points of contact.

## Product Purpose
An informational and authoritative website for a legacy yarn business. It does not sell online, but instead serves to establish trust, provide product specifications, occasionally share blogs, and capture high-value business leads.

## Brand Personality
Authoritative, established, and technical. The interface should evoke industrial precision, trust, and unwavering reliability (akin to a high-end engineering or manufacturing site).

## Anti-references
- No generic "AI slop" (floating blobs, neon glows, rounded pills).
- No consumer-style e-commerce layouts.
- No generic neon Web3 sites.
- No traditional, boring corporate layouts that feel outdated.

## Design Principles
1. **Engineered Precision**: Layouts should feel structural and precise, akin to an engineering schematic or spec sheet, not a consumer app.
2. **Authoritative Trust**: The design must communicate decades of reliability without feeling dated. 
3. **Information First**: Since products aren't sold online, the goal is clear communication of capabilities and specifications to drive offline conversion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility (clear contrast, legible text) is fine.
